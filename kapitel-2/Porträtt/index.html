Isabel LaRosa

Isabel Sofia LaRosa (born September 18, 2004), is a
Cuban-American singer, songwriter and video director
from Annapolis, Maryland. She signed to RCA Records in
2021 and released her debut EP, I'm Watching You, in 2022. She
rose to prominence after releasing her 2022 single "I'm Yours",
which went viral on the social media platform TikTok. Her
debut studio album, Raven, was released on April 18, 2025.
